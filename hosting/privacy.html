Anteroom

Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 September 2026

Anteroom turns photographs of your medical paperwork into a structured brief you can hand to a doctor. That means the app handles health information, so this policy is specific about what is collected, where it goes, and how to erase it.

The short version. Your documents and briefs are stored in your own account in the European Union and are not shared with anyone. There are no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs and no trackers in the app. You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app, and the deletion is immediate and permanent.

Who is responsible

Anteroom is operated by Dr. Ahmed Zayed ("we", "us"). For data protection purposes we are the controller of the information described below. You can reach us at support@anteroom.app.

What the app collects

CategoryWhat it isWhy
Account Email address and password, and the display name, date of birth, language and country you optionally enter during setup. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us. To sign you in and keep your briefs attached to you and nobody else.
Profiles For yourself and anyone you add: a name, a relationship, and optionally a date of birth and biological sex. So a brief says whose paperwork it is, and so the brief can note age- and sex-relevant context.
Documents The photographs of medical paperwork you choose to add — referrals, prescriptions, medication lists, discharge summaries, lab results. These are the input. Nothing is captured in the background; a page is uploaded only when you add it to a brief.
Briefs The structured text derived from those photographs: conditions, medications and dosages, allergies, questions for the doctor, and items flagged as unreadable or needing confirmation. This is the output you came for.
Subscription state Whether the Anteroom Pro entitlement is active, the product identifier, the store, and the expiry date. To unlock Pro features. We never receive your card details — the App Store or Google Play handles payment.

What the app does not collect

  • No analytics, advertising, attribution or crash-reporting SDKs are included in the app.
  • No advertising identifier, no location, no contacts, no device fingerprint.
  • No access to your photo library beyond the individual images you pick. On iOS the picker runs outside the app, so the app never sees the rest of your library.

Where it is stored

Everything is stored in Google Firebase and Google Cloud under our project. The database is in Google's eur3 multi-region and the page images and server-side functions are in europe-west1 (Belgium). Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Google Cloud.

How documents are processed

To produce a brief, the pages you added are sent to Google's Vertex AI (Gemini models) running in europe-west1 and are processed to extract the text into a structured form. Google acts as our processor for this and, under the Google Cloud Service Specific Terms, does not use this content to train its models.

The same route is used for the two other model-backed features: classifying what kind of document a page is, and translating a completed brief between English and Spanish.

Translation is deliberately constrained. Drug names, numeric doses and units are copied across unchanged rather than re-generated, so a translation cannot introduce a dose that was not in the original document.

Who else sees it

  • Google (Firebase, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Vertex AI) — our infrastructure and processing provider.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription state. It receives your Firebase user ID and the purchase details from the store. It does not receive your documents, your briefs or your email address.
  • Apple and Google — process the payment itself under their own privacy policies.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. We will disclose data if we are legally required to, and we will tell you unless we are prohibited from doing so.

How long it is kept

Briefs and page images stay in your account until you delete them or delete your account. We do not expire them on a schedule, because the point of the app is that last year's discharge summary is still there when you need it.

Deleting your account

Open the app, scroll to the bottom of the home screen and tap Delete account. Confirming erases, permanently:

  • every brief and all of its extracted content;
  • every page image you uploaded;
  • every profile, including your own;
  • your subscription entitlement record and usage counters;
  • your sign-in credential.

There is no recovery period and no copy kept for support purposes. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription — that is billed by Apple or Google and has to be cancelled in your store account.

Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the GDPR gives you the right to access, correct, export, restrict, object to, and erase your personal data. Deletion is built into the app; for anything else, write to support@anteroom.app and we will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority.

Our legal basis for processing your account data is performance of the contract between us. Health information in your documents is processed on the basis of your explicit consent, given when you add a page to a brief; you can withdraw it by deleting the brief or your account.

Children

Anteroom is not directed at children and we do not knowingly create accounts for anyone under 16. An adult account holder may add a child as a profile — for example to keep a paediatric referral — in which case the adult is the one providing that information and is responsible for it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that affects how your data is used, we will update the date at the top and describe the change in the app before it takes effect.
