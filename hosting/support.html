Support

Write to support@anteroom.app. Include what you were doing and what happened — never include a photograph of anyone's medical paperwork in an email to us.

Common questions

A brief left a dose blank

That is deliberate. If the app cannot read a value with confidence it leaves the field empty and adds an entry under flagged items rather than inventing a plausible number. Re-photograph the page in better light, or fill it in from the original document.

The brief did not generate

Check that each page is in focus and that the whole sheet is in the frame. A brief takes up to six pages; beyond that, split it into two. There is also a daily processing limit per account, which resets at midnight UTC.

I paid but Pro is not unlocked

Open the paywall and tap Restore purchases. If it still does not unlock, make sure you are signed into the same store account that made the purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google, not by us, so they have to be cancelled there — in your Apple ID subscription settings, or in Google Play. Deleting your Anteroom account does not cancel a subscription.

How do I delete my data?

Scroll to the bottom of the home screen and tap Delete account. It erases every brief, every page image, every profile and your sign-in credential, immediately and permanently. See the Privacy Policy.

Is Anteroom medical advice?

No. It reorganises documents you already have. It does not diagnose or recommend treatment, and a brief is never a reason to change a medication. Check every brief against the original paperwork and ask the clinician who prescribed it.
