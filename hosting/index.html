Anteroom

Walk in ready.

Photograph the referral, the prescription, the discharge summary. Anteroom reads them into one structured brief — conditions, medications and doses, allergies, and the questions worth asking — that you can hand to a doctor in any language they read.

It tells you what it could not read. A smudged dose is listed as unreadable and flagged for you to confirm, not filled in with something plausible. That is the whole design: an extraction that guesses is worse than no extraction at all.

What it does

  • Turns up to six photographed pages into one brief.
  • Keeps a profile for each person whose paperwork you carry.
  • Exports a PDF to hand over or email ahead.
  • Translates a finished brief between English and Spanish, copying drug names and numeric doses across untouched.
